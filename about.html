About NIST

Excellence in science and technology education.

Who We Are

Nairobi Institute of Science and Technology (NIST) specialises in Information Technology and computing. We offer Certificate, Diploma, Degree and Masters programmes for Kenya’s digital economy.

Vision

To be a premier institute of academic excellence in science and technology with a practical, industry-oriented focus.

Mission

To promote knowledge, technical skills and character so graduates contribute effectively in a changing technological world.

Core Values

  • Excellence — high standards in teaching and support
  • Integrity — ethical conduct and accountability
  • Innovation — continuous improvement
  • Inclusivity — diverse learners welcome
  • Industry Relevance — employer-aligned curricula
